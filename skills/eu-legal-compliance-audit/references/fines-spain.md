## Reductions and alternatives (important for the "minimum" column)

- **LSSI Art. 39 bis.2 — warning instead of fine**: for leve or grave LSSI infractions (cookies, legal notice, spam) when the offender has not been sanctioned or warned before, the authority may issue an *apercibimiento* and require correction within a deadline (typically one month). Use **0 €** as the minimum for first-time LSSI findings.
- **LSSI Art. 39 bis.1 — moderation**: the fine can be set using the next lower severity range when culpability or illegality is significantly reduced (e.g. quick correction).
- **Ley 39/2015 Art. 85 — payment reductions** (used systematically by the AEPD): 20% for acknowledging responsibility + 20% for voluntary payment before resolution, cumulative → up to 40% off the proposed fine.
- **GDPR reprimand**: for minor infractions or where a fine would be disproportionate for a natural person, a reprimand (Art. 58(2)(b)) can replace the fine.

## Prescription (how long exposure lasts)

- LOPDGDD: muy graves 3 years, graves 2 years, leves 1 year.
- LSSI: muy graves 3 years, graves 2 years, leves 6 months.
- Continued infractions (a cookie banner that stays wrong) keep running while the conduct lasts — prescription doesn't start until it stops.

## Civil and other exposure (not in the fines total)

- GDPR Art. 82 damages: individual claims in Spanish courts exist but are usually modest; collective actions by consumer associations are possible.
- Competitors can sue for unfair competition (Ley 3/1991) based on breaches of these rules.
- Lawyer fees for defending a sanction procedure and the cost of remediation are separate.
